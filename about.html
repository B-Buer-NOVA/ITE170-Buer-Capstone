My Story

Berry Buer

Hi! I'm Berry Buer! I'm taking ITE 170 for my Web Design and Development Career Certificate! I came to NOVA because I wanted to further my art eduction without a big price tag. I've definitely gotten to explore and learn more about art during my time here! My current study of art and programming feel very close knit to me, and multimedia is the natural intersection of both fields!

I find great satisfaction in creating websites! Frankly, I'd love to make a few personal website or blogs about my artwork or interests; alas, it's hard to find the time. I'm hoping that a wide scope of artistic and programming skills will land me a good, stable career in the future!

As an artist with about half a dozen hands on hobbies; my personal or creative drive is to make things myself. I love coding and creating things myself! Below is a quick list of all the hobbies I've gotten to engage in this past semester!

  • Yarn Work: crochet, tunisian crochet, knitting
  • Sewing by hand and by machine
  • Whittling or wood carving
  • Painting (acrylic and gouache)
  • Digital Art!

My learning journey

Summer 2023 – NOVA Bridge Program

What brought you to IT?

During Summer 2023, I joined the 'NOVA Bridge Program' which offered high schoolers the chance to explore the Information Technology or Computer Science fields at the NOVA campuses. I took the IT Program, and I discovered a love for coding and creating websites!

Fall 2024

My Start at NOVA

I graduated high school as a Magna Cum Laude in May 2026, and I started attending NOVA in August for the Fall 2024 semester. I enrolled into the Visual Arts Associate's program as well as career certificates in Application Programming and Web Design and Development.

Summer 2026

A Completed Certificate!

I finished my Application Programming Career Certificate in Summer 2026! I finally received the physical certificate a week ago. I learned Python coding, and I'm interested in learning JAVA as well (even though it wouldn't count to any credit hours or requirements).

Fall 2026 – Current Semester

ITE 170 – Multimedia Software

I've taken ITD 110 and ITD 210 previously, so this course has been great for focusing on the artistic side of web creation! I just need to take ITE 270 and ITP 140 or ITP 225 to complete my Web Design Certificate. I'm also taking 2D Design, 3D Design, and Painting 1 this semester, so I've been swimming in art design!

Beyond NOVA

While the future is never set in stone, I plan to attend Radford University for a Bachelor's in Information Technology and a minor in Graphic Design. I want to have a strong base in both programming and design as I enter the field of web development.
